Questions

Good to know.

Your co-op keeps the books. Fundo shows you what they record, and every payment goes straight to them.

Does Fundo hold my money?

No. Fundo shows the records your co-op shares. When you pay, the money goes from your GCash, Maya or bank to your co-op.

My co-op isn't on Fundo yet. Can I still sign up?

Join the list with your mobile number. We'll text you when your co-op connects, and you can tell your co-op you want it.

Can I link more than one co-op?

Yes. Add each membership number. Every co-op confirms its own membership before your records appear.

What if my phone has no fingerprint sensor?

Use a 6-digit passcode instead. You can turn fingerprint sign-in on or off from the Me tab.

Do SMS alerts need mobile data?

No. SMS and MMS reminders arrive as regular text messages, so they reach you even without data.
